Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cursed Video Bot handles information when the application is used.

1. Information processed

Cursed Video Bot may process video files and related media selected by the user for the purpose of generating short-form video content. The desktop application is designed to process these files locally unless the user explicitly enables a service that requires transmission.

2. TikTok authorization

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application uses TikTok's official authorization mechanisms. The application may receive authorization tokens and related information required to perform the TikTok functionality that the user has authorized and that has been approved or enabled for the application.

3. How information is used

  • To generate and process videos requested by the user.
  • To maintain the authorized TikTok connection.
  • To perform enabled TikTok API operations requested by the user.
  • To troubleshoot and maintain the application where necessary.

4. Sharing

Cursed Video Bot does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide an explicitly requested service, to operate an authorized TikTok integration, to comply with applicable law, or where the user otherwise directs or authorizes the transfer.

5. Data retention

Source and generated video files are intended to remain under the user's control on the device running the application. Authorization information may be retained as needed to operate the authorized integration and may be revoked by the user through the relevant account or application controls.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User choices and account connection

Users choose whether to connect a TikTok account. Users can revoke access through the applicable TikTok account controls and can stop using the integration at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page.
