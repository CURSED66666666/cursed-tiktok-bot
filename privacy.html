Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Information processed

The application may process video files selected by the user so that it can generate short-form video content. These files are processed on the user's computer unless the user explicitly uses a service that requires transmission elsewhere.

TikTok account authorization

If the user connects a TikTok account, Cursed TikTok Bot uses TikTok's official authorization mechanisms and only requests permissions necessary for enabled TikTok functionality. Access tokens and related authorization information are used to perform authorized functions.

Sharing

Cursed TikTok Bot does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide an explicitly requested service, comply with law, or operate the authorized TikTok integration.

Data retention

Generated videos and local source files remain under the user's control on their computer. Authorization information is retained only as needed for the application to operate and may be deleted by the user.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes. The updated version will be published on this page.
